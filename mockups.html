Direction B — Editorial Bold

Light and confident. Big serif headlines, oversized numerals, a strong PwC rule. Reads like a well-made report rather than a dashboard. Calmer than A, holds up far better across a long read, prints and screenshots cleanly into a deck.

Direction C — Split Reveal

The contrast itself becomes the interface. Old on the left in grey, frontier on the right in colour, side by side rather than behind a toggle. Makes the argument in one glance and is the most obviously interactive — the divider can be dragged to wipe between them.

Same process. Two different days.

Every step on the left still exists on the right — it is just no longer a person doing it by hand.
